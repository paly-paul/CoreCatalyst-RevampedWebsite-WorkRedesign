Where the Time Goes

The hidden cost of manual skills data management

HR operations teams spend a disproportionate amount of their time on data work that adds no strategic value. Pulling exports, reconciling formats, chasing managers for updates, and building reports that are out of date before they land.

WorkRedesign automates the ingestion and structuring layer - freeing your team to focus on the analysis and advisory work that actually moves the business forward.

Data extraction and reconciliation~40% of HR ops time
Chasing manager updates and sign-offs~25% of HR ops time
Building and formatting reports~20% of HR ops time
Compliance and audit preparation~15% of HR ops time

Based on interviews with HR operations leads across financial services, healthcare, logistics, and manufacturing organisations. The mix of effort shifts by sector - but the manual data burden is consistent across Finance, Procurement, SCM, and Operations functions.

What You Get

What changes for your team

From manual data wrangling to a governed, automated pipeline - with reporting that is always current and audit-ready.

Automated ingestion from all your connected systems

No more manual exports and reconciliation. WorkRedesign ingests data from your HRIS, LMS, and performance systems on a configurable schedule - and surfaces discrepancies for review rather than silently writing them to the record. Your team spends time on exceptions, not on the routine data work.

A single governed skills record

One source of truth for skills data across your organisation - replacing the spreadsheets, disconnected system exports, and informal tracking that currently fragment your picture. Every record has a clear provenance trail.

Configurable validation workflows

Adjudication workflows are configured to match your existing HR governance processes - not to add a new process on top. You control who validates what, at what cadence, and with what escalation path.

Audit-ready reporting, always current

Reports generated from a live, governed record - not assembled manually from stale exports. Every report is sourced, timestamped, and traceable. When compliance or audit asks for evidence, you have it.

Manager workflows that actually get completed

The adjudication interface is built for manager adoption - fast, structured, and surfaced at the right moments. Completion rates are tracked and escalated within your governance workflow so you are not chasing people manually.

Data quality monitoring

Continuous monitoring of record completeness, staleness, and validation status. You know at any time which parts of your skills record are current and reliable - and which need attention.

Security and Compliance

Built for enterprise compliance from day one

Designed for organisations where data governance and regulatory compliance are not optional - they are the starting point.

🌐 Data residency and sovereignty

Your data stays in your environment. WorkRedesign connects to your systems securely but does not copy your employee data to our infrastructure. Data residency requirements are met by design, not configuration.

No employee data stored in WorkRedesign infrastructure
Supports GDPR, UK GDPR, and regional data residency requirements
Data processing agreements available for all enterprise engagements

🔒 Access control and audit logging

Granular role-based access control. Every read, write, and adjudication decision is logged with user identity, timestamp, and context. Audit logs are immutable and exportable for compliance review.

Role-based access control with configurable permission sets
Immutable audit log for every data access and modification event
SSO integration with your existing identity provider

⚖️ Bias monitoring and fairness governance

The adjudication process includes bias monitoring at the point of manager review - flagging patterns that may indicate inconsistent validation across demographic groups, before they enter the record.

Statistical bias detection across adjudication decisions
Configurable flagging thresholds for your compliance requirements
Bias monitoring reports available for HR and compliance review

📄 Employee rights and data access

Employees have the right to see their own validated skills record and to raise disputes through the governed process. Subject access requests and data deletion requests are handled within the platform workflow.

Employee self-service view of their own skills record
Structured dispute and appeal process with audit trail
Subject access request and right-to-erasure workflows
