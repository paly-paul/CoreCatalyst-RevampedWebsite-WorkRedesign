How It Works

Designed to fit into how you already manage

The adjudication process is built around manager workflows - not bolted on top of them. It takes minutes per employee, not hours.

1

Review AI-generated skill profiles for your team

WorkRedesign pre-populates profiles for each of your reports based on data from your HRIS, LMS, and performance systems. You see a first-pass view that already captures much of what you know - and you tell us where it is right and where it is not.

🕑 Typically 5-10 minutes per employee
2

Accept, add evidence, or flag disputes

For each skill claim, you can accept it with a note, add evidence that supports it (a project outcome, a peer observation, a specific piece of work), or flag it for review if you disagree. No skill enters the record without your sign-off.

🔒 Every decision is audit-logged with your name and rationale
3

See the validated view of your team

Once the adjudication is complete, you get a clean, validated view of your team's capabilities - which you can use immediately in planning, development conversations, and internal mobility decisions. It is your knowledge, made searchable and shareable.

🌟 Role-gap view and career lattices generated automatically
4

Keep it current as your team evolves

Skills records are not a one-time exercise. WorkRedesign is configured to surface update prompts at the right moments - after a project completes, after a performance review, when someone moves roles. You stay in control without a constant admin burden.

📈 Configurable update cadence that fits your review rhythm
