The Real Questions

The questions CHROs are asking right now

Every CHRO we speak to is grappling with the same set of challenges. WorkRedesign is built to answer them - with evidence, not assumption.

"How exposed is our workforce to AI automation - really?"

We build an automation charter from your verified skills record, identifying which tasks are genuinely AI-automatable by role - not a generic industry overlay. In a typical 500-person organisation, we find that accounts payable clerks, procurement coordinators, logistics administrators, and HR generalists often carry more task-level AI exposure than senior technical specialists. Knowing this by function, not just by headcount, is what makes the conversation board-ready.

"Can I trust the skills data I have well enough to act on it?"

Probably not yet. Most organisations have skills data that is self-reported, system-inferred, or simply old. WorkRedesign puts every skill claim through a human-validated reconciliation process before it enters the record - so you can act on it.

"How do I show the board we are ready for the AI era?"

We produce a board-ready intelligence export built from your verified workforce data. Formatted for executive audiences. Sourced transparently. Something you can actually present with confidence.

"Where do we find the talent we need - inside or outside?"

Career lattices built from your verified skills map show exactly what internal mobility is possible - horizontal, vertical, and cross-functional. A procurement coordinator with unrecognised contract management skills may be the strongest internal candidate for a category manager role. A warehouse operative already doing daily route planning may be the next logistics coordinator. An AP specialist with supplier negotiation experience may already be functioning as a junior procurement analyst. Most organisations have more internal depth than their HRIS shows. We make it visible.

"Our job descriptions are years out of date. Where do we start?"

Redefined job descriptions are one of the four core outputs of the WorkRedesign process. They are generated directly from your verified skills record - skills-based, AI-era ready, and grounded in what the role actually requires today. This includes professional roles in Finance, HR, and Procurement, as well as operational and frontline roles in Logistics, Customer Service, and Warehouse functions where skills-based clarity matters just as much for deployment and development decisions.

"If I invest in this, how do I know it will stick?"

We start with a 90-day proof of concept scoped to one business unit. You see real outputs - a validated skills map, career lattices, an automation charter - before you decide whether to scale. No platform commitment before you have seen results.
