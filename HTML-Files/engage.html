How we work together

Start with a conversation, not a contract

We do not ask you to watch a demo, fill out a lead form, or go through a sales process before we talk. The first step is a direct conversation with a WorkRedesign founder about what you are trying to solve.

If we are the right fit, we will tell you. If we are not, we will tell you that too.

We do not follow up with automated emails. After the Discovery Call, you will hear from the same person you spoke to.
The discovery call

What the 30 minutes looks like

No slides. No product tour. A structured conversation covering the three things we need to understand before we can tell you whether WorkRedesign is right for you.

01

Your workforce challenge

We start by understanding what is driving this conversation. Is it board pressure around AI readiness? A restructure that exposed gaps in your skills data? An HRIS migration that stalled because nobody trusted the underlying data? The shape of the challenge determines everything else.

02

Your current data landscape

Where does skills data live today? What systems are in scope, what condition is the data in, and how much line manager engagement can you realistically get? This tells us whether a 90-day engagement is feasible or whether there is remediation work needed first.

03

Fit and next steps

We give you a frank assessment. If this is a good fit, we describe what the scoping workshop would look like and what a Proof of Concept engagement could cover. If it is not, we say so and, if possible, suggest a better starting point.

The full journey

From first call to operational foundation

Here is every step from the Discovery Call through to a delivered, governed skills foundation your organisation can run independently.

1
Week 0

Discovery Call (30 minutes, free)

A direct conversation with a WorkRedesign founder. We cover your challenge, your data landscape, and whether the timing is right. You walk away with a clear sense of fit and, if relevant, what the next step would look like.

2
Week 1-2

Scoping workshop

A half-day working session with your HR leadership team. We map the population you want to cover, the source systems we need to connect, and the specific outcomes your organisation is trying to achieve. This session produces the inputs for the fixed-price proposal.

  • Population sizing and function selection
  • Source system inventory and data quality assessment
  • Success criteria definition with your team
  • Risk and readiness assessment
3
Week 2-3

Statement of Work

We send you a fixed-price proposal covering the full engagement: scope, deliverables, milestones, team, and price. Nothing is hidden. You review it, challenge it, and when you are satisfied, you sign it. We do not start work until you have.

4
Weeks 4-8

Data ingestion and reconciliation

We connect to your source systems, ingest the skills-adjacent data across your LMS, performance system, HRIS, and job architecture, and begin the reconciliation process. Our platform surfaces conflicts, gaps, and duplicates for human review.

  • Automated ingestion from all in-scope systems
  • Conflict detection and flagging
  • Initial skills map against your catalogue
  • Gap analysis by role and function
5
Weeks 6-10

Manager adjudication

Line managers review the inferred skills for their direct reports. They accept, dispute, or add evidence through a structured interface designed to take less than 20 minutes per person. Our team facilitates the rollout and handles escalations.

6
Weeks 10-12

Output delivery

We deliver the full output package: validated skills map, redefined job descriptions, career lattice, automation charter, and board-ready summary. Data is exported into your HRIS in your preferred format.

  • Validated skills map with evidence links
  • Updated job descriptions for all in-scope roles
  • Career lattice showing internal mobility pathways
  • Automation exposure charter by role family
  • Board-ready skills and capability summary
  • HRIS-ready export in your format
7
Post-delivery

Governance handover

We hand over the governance model, refresh schedule, and owner assignments that keep your skills data current. Your team can run this independently. If you want ongoing support, the Advisory Retainer is available. If not, the foundation is yours to build on.

Good fit

WorkRedesign works well when

  • ✓You have a workforce of 500 or more employees and meaningful variation in roles across functions
  • ✓Skills data currently lives in at least two separate systems that do not speak to each other
  • ✓HR leadership has executive sponsorship for this work and can bring line managers into the process
  • ✓You want a validated, governed output rather than another taxonomy or tagging exercise
  • ✓The board or executive team has asked a question about AI readiness or workforce capability that HR cannot currently answer
  • ✓You need something that is HRIS-agnostic and works with your existing stack rather than replacing it
Not the right fit

We are probably not the answer if

  • ✗You are looking for a skills tagging tool your team self-configures without external support
  • ✗Your organisation has fewer than 200 employees, where the value of a full reconciliation process is hard to justify
  • ✗The initiative does not have senior HR or business sponsorship and line managers are unlikely to engage
  • ✗You need an immediate, this-quarter solution, and 90 days is too long to wait for the deliverable
  • ✗You have already invested in a skills platform and need help using it, rather than building the data layer underneath it
